The piece

One canvas, a hundred models.

Fusion is the AI companion for makers who write, plan, and ship — one canvas, a hundred models, tuned to the shape of your voice.

The page is built around a live-feeling blend demo: three voice memos become a one-page investor note, fused from three models, with a tone-match readout. The type is Fraunces set large and italic against near-black, with a molten copper accent that only ever appears once per viewport.

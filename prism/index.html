The piece

A whole studio, in your pocket.

Prism is a phone-shaped studio for people who make music between train stops. Loop, layer, whisper into it — send it before the doubt catches up.

The page leads with an iOS mock playing a live loop, then four capability beats: sing/hum/tap as an instrument, an endless royalty-clear loop bank, one-tap send to stems or MIDI, and AI that stays quiet — it suggests chords and matches tempo, but never generates the good parts for you.

The piece

Four things, done slowly.

A small bureau, and small on purpose. Every project passes through the same four hands — no account teams, no hand-offs, no rooms where the work is nowhere in particular. The bureau thinks in objects: an issue, a system, a spread, a home page.

The digital work reads like print. The print reads like it has always been there. Cream paper, a black grotesque, and a Swiss grid held to a single accent colour across the whole edition.

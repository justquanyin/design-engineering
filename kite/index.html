The piece

Closer to a wooden desk in a bright room.

Kite is small — deliberately, almost stubbornly small. It runs on your machine, saves to plain Markdown, and doesn't know what a "workspace" is. There is a cursor, a caret, and one file at a time. Everything you write is yours before you press save.

Most writing apps ship a room full of furniture and hope you'll write anyway. Kite is closer to a wooden desk in a bright room. There is a lamp, if you need it, and nothing else.
